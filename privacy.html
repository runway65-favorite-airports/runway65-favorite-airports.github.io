Privacy policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

What Runway is

Runway is a private planning app that a small group of invited people use to read one shared Google Sheet in a friendlier layout. It is run by an individual, not a company. It has no public sign-up.

What Google data Runway accesses

  • Your Google account name and email address, so the app can show who is signed in.
  • Up to two spreadsheets that you choose with Google's file chooser, including their contents. Runway cannot open any other file in your Drive.
  • If you have edit access to a chosen spreadsheet, Runway can add or change rows in it, but only when you take an action in the app, such as checking off a task or recording a decision. It never changes anything on its own.
  • Whether you have permission to edit that spreadsheet, so the app can show a "View only" notice where it applies.

How the data is used

The data is shown to you on your own screen, in your browser. Changes you make go straight from your browser to the spreadsheet you chose. Runway has no server and no database. Nothing from your Google account or the spreadsheet is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What is stored

  • Your sign-in token is kept in the browser's memory only and disappears when you close the tab or sign out.
  • The browser saves the chosen spreadsheets' IDs and names on your device so that you do not have to choose it each time. You can remove it with "Change sheet" or by clearing the site's data.
  • Nothing else is stored.

Fonts

The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts. As with any web font, that means Google receives your IP address and browser details when you open a page. Runway does not add anything else to that request.

Sharing, selling and advertising

Runway does not share, sell or transfer Google user data. It does not show advertising, use analytics, or build profiles.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Runway's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can stop Runway's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions by selecting Runway and removing access. Signing out in the app also revokes the current token.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the issues page of this site's repository on GitHub.
